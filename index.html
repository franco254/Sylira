Sylira Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Sylira is made by coreloopdev.

What stays on your phone

Sylira plays audio and video files that are already on your device. Your library, playlists, favourites, listening history and settings are stored only on your device. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We run no servers and receive none of your data.

Online lookups (optional, off by default)

If you turn on "Online covers & lyrics" in Settings, Sylira sends a song's title, artist, album name and length (never the audio file) to third-party services to find cover art and lyrics: MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive (musicbrainz.org, coverartarchive.org) and LRCLIB (lrclib.net). These requests carry no account or advertising ID. Those services see your IP address like any website and have their own policies. Turn the setting off at any time to stop the requests.

Permissions

Music and video access: to list and play your media. Notifications: playback controls. Foreground service: background playback. Modify system settings: only if you choose to set a trimmed song as your ringtone or notification sound. Internet: only for the optional lookups above.

Backups

A backup is a file you save and restore yourself; Sylira does not upload it anywhere.

Retention and deletion

Data lives on your device until you clear it or uninstall Sylira. We hold nothing on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

Sylira is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version ships with an app update and the date above changes.
